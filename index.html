For Every Filipino · Wherever You Are

Seven Thousand Islands.
One Expedition.

The islands did not wait to be discovered.
They rose from the Pacific, one by one —
born of fire, shaped by tide,
ancient beyond naming, alive beyond counting.
They have always been here.
And so have you.

You come from one of the most extraordinary places on Earth. Seven thousand islands at the edge of the Pacific. 52,177 known species — eagles, tarsiers, whale sharks, forests that outlasted the ice age, reefs so alive they defy counting. Traditions and languages spoken by no one else on the planet. A history that stretches back further than most of the world knows.

This is not just a place. This is who you are.

Expedition PH is an invitation — to every Filipino, whether you were born on these islands or carry them with you across the world — to go deeper into the land, the life, and the people that made you.
