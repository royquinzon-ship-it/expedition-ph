Credits & Sources

All wildlife records, conservation data, and geographic information on this site are drawn from verified scientific sources. This page credits the researchers, naturalists, and institutions behind them.

◆   Accuracy Commitment   ◆

Our Standard

How We Verify

Every species listed on Expedition PH has been cross-referenced against at least one verifiable scientific source — field observation records, institutional checklists, or peer-reviewed literature. If a species cannot be confirmed for a specific location, it is not listed. Conservation status follows the IUCN Red List. Where records conflict, the most conservative interpretation is used.

Primary Sources

Wildlife Records

Citizen Science Platform
iNaturalist

Species occurrence records and observation data for Philippine provinces. Used to verify presence of species at the provincial level against community-verified observations.

Visit iNaturalist ›
Regional Bird Checklist
Avibase — Bird Checklists of the World

Provincial and regional bird checklists for the Philippines following Howard & Moore 4th edition taxonomy. Used to validate species presence and enumerate threatened species per province.

Visit Avibase ›
Field Birding Records
Alain Pascua — Birds of Ilocos Norte

Field observation records documenting bird species at specific sites across Ilocos Norte — Gabu (Laoag City), Pasuquin, Adams, Bangui, and Pagudpud. Used as a primary verification source for the province.

Visit alainpascua.com ›
Birding Survey
Wild Bird Club of the Philippines

First Region I Birding Caravan (2025) — field survey documenting bird species across Ilocos Sur and La Union, including records from Bessang Pass. Primary verification source for Ilocos Sur wildlife.

Visit birdwatch.ph ›
Conservation Status
IUCN Red List of Threatened Species

All conservation status designations — Critically Endangered, Endangered, Vulnerable, Near Threatened, Least Concern — follow the IUCN Red List. Statuses are updated as new assessments are published.

Visit IUCN Red List ›
Protected Areas
DENR — Dept. of Environment & Natural Resources

Protected area designations and management status follow DENR records under the National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS), including updates from the Expanded NIPAS Act of 2018.

Visit DENR ›

Images

Photo Credits

Photographs on this site are sourced from the project's own collection and are used for educational and non-commercial purposes. Species and location photographs are selected to accurately represent the subject — where a dedicated photograph is not yet available, a representative image from the same region or habitat type is used as a temporary placeholder.

If you are a photographer whose work appears here without attribution, or if you would like to contribute verified field photographs of Philippine species and landscapes, please get in touch via the About page.
